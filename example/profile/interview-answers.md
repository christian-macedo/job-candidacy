# Interview answer bank

Answers built from evidence atoms in `profile/master-profile.md`. Nothing invented. Written to
be spoken, not read. Promoted here out of debriefs once an answer has proved it works at more
than one company.

**Voice convention:** "I" for decisions, framing, funding and accountability. "The team" for
implementation and delivery. Interviewers discount "we" because it hides which one you were.

---

## Q: Tell me about a time you improved an on-call rotation.

**Atoms used:** NWL-M-02.

The shape: what was wrong, what everyone wanted to do, what you did instead, the number.

> The rotation was paging someone most nights. The instinct in the room was more process, and I
> argued the opposite: most of what fired described nothing a human could act on. We deleted
> those alerts, and routed what was left to the service that owned it rather than to whoever was
> holding the pager. Weekly pages went from 90 to 22. The part I would defend is the deleting,
> because it is the part that felt risky at the time.

Follow-up it invites: "how did you know it was safe to delete?" Answer with the review process,
not with confidence.

## Q: Tell me about an underperformer.

**Atoms used:** NWL-M-04.

> A senior engineer whose output had dropped and whose reviews had gone quiet. I wrote down what
> good looked like, with dates, and we worked a nine week plan. The thing I did differently from
> the last time I handled one of these was to say out loud in week one that this was a formal
> plan, rather than hoping the hint landed. They stayed, and led the biggest project of the
> following quarter.

Do not tell this story as a rescue. The interviewer is listening for whether the plan was real.

## Q: Build versus buy.

**Atoms used:** NWL-M-05.

> Route optimisation. Building it was more interesting and I argued for buying anyway, because
> the differentiator was our data and not the solver. It freed roughly fourteen engineer-months.
> I would make the same call again, and the thing I would do differently is write the reversal
> criteria down at the time instead of a year later.
