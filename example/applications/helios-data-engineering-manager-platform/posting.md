# Engineering Manager, Platform Reliability · Helios Data

_Fictional posting, written for the example. Captured 2026-08-11._

Helios Data runs an observability platform. The Platform Reliability team owns the ingestion
path: the services that accept customer telemetry, buffer it, and hand it to storage.

## What you will do

- Lead a team of 6 to 9 engineers owning ingestion and the on-call rotation for it.
- Own reliability targets for a path that takes tens of millions of events per day.
- Partner with product on a roadmap that is mostly platform work with no direct user interface.
- Hire. We expect the team to grow by 3 engineers this year.

## What we are looking for

- 3+ years managing backend or platform engineers, including hiring and performance management.
- A background as a backend engineer, deep enough to review a design and disagree with it.
- Experience running a production on-call rotation and improving it.
- Working knowledge of distributed data pipelines: queues, backpressure, at-least-once delivery.
- Comfort with Postgres at scale.

## Nice to have

- Kubernetes.
- Experience with a build-versus-buy decision at the platform layer.
- Go.

Lisbon, hybrid, 2 days a week in the office. We sponsor for EU work authorization.
