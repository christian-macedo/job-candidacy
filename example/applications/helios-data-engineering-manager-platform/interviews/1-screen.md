# Round 1: Recruiter screen · Helios Data

**When:** Thursday 20 August 2026, 15:00 WEST, 30 minutes, phone.
**Who:** Talent partner. Name not given in the invite.
**Resume they hold:** `submitted/2026-08-14/resume.pdf`.

This round is a filter, not an evaluation. It decides nothing except whether the next call
happens, and its second job is intelligence: who the hiring manager is, how big the team really
is, and what rounds 2 and 3 actually cover.

## 1. The two-minute answer

Asked as "tell me about yourself". It is the only answer with a rehearsed shape.

> I run the platform team behind shipment tracking at Northwind Logistics. Seven engineers, and
> the ingestion path they own is what customers judge the product on. The work I would point at
> first is the on-call rotation: weekly pages went from 90 to 22, and we got there by deleting
> alerts rather than adding process. Before managing I was a backend engineer for eight years,
> and I still review designs. I am looking for the same problem at a company where reliability
> is the product rather than a supporting concern, which is why your ingestion team caught my eye.

Follow-up it invites: "what did you delete?" Have two concrete examples ready.

## 2. Work authorization

> I am a US citizen, so I would need sponsorship for Portugal. Your posting says you sponsor for
> EU work authorization, so I am asking whether that applies to this role specifically and what
> the timeline usually looks like.

Ask it as a question with a shape, not as an open request.

## 3. Compensation

> I am looking at the Lisbon band for an engineering manager at this level. Rather than anchor
> on my current number, which is a US one and not comparable, I would rather hear the band you
> have budgeted for this role and tell you whether it works.

## 4. Where the gap is

Name it before they find it. Kubernetes is the one.

> My Kubernetes experience is working knowledge rather than depth. I led a database migration
> that ran on it, but I have never owned a cluster. If the role needs cluster ownership on day
> one, that is worth knowing now.

## What to collect for the next round

1. The hiring manager's name and what they run.
2. The real team size today, and how much of the growth to three is already approved.
3. What rounds 2 and 3 actually cover, and who sits on the panel.
4. The compensation band for this level in Lisbon.
5. Why the headcount exists: growth, backfill, or a rotation nobody wants to carry.
6. Whether the sponsorship route is one they have run before.

## Questions to ask them

1. What is the ingestion path's reliability like today, in their words?
2. What would the first six months be judged on?
3. What is the on-call rotation like right now, honestly?
4. How does this team decide between building and buying?
