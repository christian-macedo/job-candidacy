# Debrief, round 1: Recruiter screen · Helios Data

**When it happened:** Thursday 20 August 2026, 15:00 WEST, 28 minutes, phone.
**Who:** Talent partner, platform hiring.
**Outcome:** Advanced. Round 2 to be scheduled for the first week of September.

## What they asked

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why are you looking, and why Lisbon?
3. How big is the team you run now, and how much of it did you hire?
4. Walk me through how you improved an on-call rotation.
5. Work authorization, asked directly.
6. Compensation expectations.
7. Notice period.

## What was learned

- The team is 6 engineers today, not the 6 to 9 the posting implies. **stated**
- The 6 to 9 range in the posting is the target after this year's hiring. **stated**
- The hiring manager is the director of platform and runs three teams. **stated**
- Round 2 is with that director, 60 minutes, experience and management scenarios. **stated**
- Round 3 is a panel: system design, a technical deep dive on the resume, and team fit. **stated**
- The on-call rotation is the reason the headcount exists. The recruiter called it "the thing the team complains about". **stated**
- They have sponsored for this route before and quoted roughly 10 weeks. **stated**
- The band was given as a range they described as "competitive for Lisbon" without a number. **stated**
- The reliability framing throughout suggests the director was told to stabilise before growing. **inferred**
- Nobody mentioned Kubernetes once, which reads as it genuinely being nice-to-have. **inferred**

## What went badly

The compensation answer went long. The band question came back at me and I filled the silence
instead of waiting. Next time: ask, then stop talking.

The Kubernetes boundary landed well and cost nothing. Keep naming it first.

## Feeds forward

- Round 2 is with the director, so the management scenarios matter more than the technical ones.
  Prep the underperformer turnaround and the build-versus-buy decision, neither of which has been
  used yet.
- The rotation is the sore point. Round 2 should open on it.
- Ask the director directly what "stabilise" means to them, since that framing is inferred and
  not stated.
- Confirm the band with a number before round 3.
