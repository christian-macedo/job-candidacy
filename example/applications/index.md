# Applications

One row per role. This is the answer to "which resume did I send them, and when?"

**Status:** `drafted` → `applied` → `screen` → `hm-round` → `final-round` → `offer` · or
`rejected`, `withdrawn`, `skipped`, `stale` (no reply after ~3 weeks).

The three interview statuses are the three rounds: `screen` is the recruiter call, `hm-round`
the hiring manager, `final-round` the system design, deep technical and team-fit panel. Put the
date and duration in the status cell. Prep and debrief for each round live in
`applications/<slug>/interviews/`.
